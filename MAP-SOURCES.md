# Map sources

Country outlines in `MapData.js` come from [Natural Earth 1:50m Admin 0 countries](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/50m-cultural-vectors/50m-admin-0-countries-2/), retrieved on 2026-09-16 from the [Natural Earth vector repository](https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/geojson/ne_50m_admin_0_countries.geojson). Natural Earth data is [public domain](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/).

The bundled subset includes Norway, Sweden, Finland and Denmark, plus neighboring Germany for context. Coordinates are rounded to four decimal places; only exterior rings are retained (the four covered countries keep every island's outline, since they are all part of the forecast coverage area; Germany keeps only its largest outline for context). Norway's outline excludes its remote Arctic territories (Svalbard and Jan Mayen), which sit outside MET Norway's Nowcast coverage area. City markers are approximate city centers for navigation, not address lookup results. The map is a regional overview, not a precise coverage boundary or a live rain overlay.
